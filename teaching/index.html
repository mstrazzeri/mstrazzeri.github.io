Other Teaching Activities

From 2023 to 2026, I contributed to practice-oriented teaching in social work at Bern University of Applied Sciences, including quantitative social research methods, research workshops, and debt counselling. I have also supervised Bachelor's and Master's theses at Bern University of Applied Sciences and the University of Bern. During my undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral studies, I taught tutorials in statistics, econometrics, and economic theory at the Universities of Giessen, Wisconsin–Milwaukee, and Konstanz.
